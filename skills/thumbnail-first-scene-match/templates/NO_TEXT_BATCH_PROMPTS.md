# PROMPT BUILDERS: 3-5 THUMBNAILS NO TEXT

Các bản sau là khung cho công cụ tạo ảnh. **Hãy chuyển đặc điểm thật của khúc gỗ vào mọi prompt**. Không chèn số đo và không mạo nhận kích thước là sự thật. Nếu ảnh gốc chỉ có cảnh cẩu thì phần trailer là tái dựng.

## MASTER CONSTRAINT (chèn nguyên văn vào MỌI prompt)

Use the user's supplied photograph as the ONLY visual identity reference for the log. Preserve its distinct irregular cut-end silhouette, old radial cracks, authentic dark black-brown rugged bark, weathered wood colors, knots and taper. Exaggerate perceived scale to astonishing, monumentally oversized proportions for a cinematic thumbnail, while maintaining a physically coherent transport scene. The enormous trunk MUST be fully supported by saddles on an extra-long multi-axle heavy haul trailer visibly CONNECTED to an actual tractor truck. The trailer must visibly carry the trunk, not hover nearby. Show tiny workers and believable sawmill infrastructure for scale. Photorealistic professional industrial documentary aesthetic, dramatic perspective, crisp natural textures, plausible wheels, restraints and truck chassis. One standalone image, 16:9. NO TEXT OF ANY KIND ANYWHERE IN THE IMAGE: no letters, no words, no numbers, no measurements, no writing on bark, no subtitles, no arrows, no labels, no banners, no logos, no watermarks, no readable signs or plates, no infographic panels, no split-screen collage. Never create a rainbow-colored decorative wood surface, never change the log into a slab, and never make the crane the load-bearing subject instead of the truck. No invented specifications. If the image is a reconstruction, do not present it as evidence of an actual event.

## 4 ý tưởng mẫu cho cùng một câu chuyện vận tải

**A / INSIDE THE YARD, MONUMENTAL END-GRAIN HERO**: low rear three-quarter lens close to enormous weathered log cross-section, deep visible trailer and connected cab, worker at side, securing chains; show truck already carrying load, not suspended. Visual hook: wood dominates foreground.

**B / WIDE SAWMILL ARRIVAL**: high wide diagonal shot covering the whole exceptional transport convoy entering an industrial yard, giant log clearly on multi-axle trailer, workers and buildings provide scale. Visual hook: full-vehicle scale in environment. This is a different action/story beat from A.

**C / ROAD-LEVEL ENGINEERING TRANSPORT**: low side-profile composition where the rows of axles and the trunk's startling diameter define the scene, believable tie-downs, support saddles, connected cab, working crew guiding a slow turn on an industrial lane. Visual hook: engineering complexity, not just the cut surface.

**D / FRONT-THREE-QUARTER MANEUVER**: tractor cab prominent in near foreground, gigantic trunk visually looming behind on ultra-long trailer, vehicle slowly negotiating a tight factory entrance, guiding workers tiny. Visual hook: truck and maneuver. NOT a rear-closeup like A.

**E / UNLOADING READY (optional fifth)**: trailer is stopped at unloading point with huge log still resting and fully supported by trailer, crane cable slack/being prepared overhead, crew planning unloading. Hook is the tense pre-unloading moment, not a suspended tree. Only use if video can substantiate it.

## SELF-CRITIQUE BEFORE DELIVERY

1. Does this picture independently show a truck hauling the same log? If not: BLOCK.
2. Is there even a single legible word, numeral, measurement, arrow, logo, or watermark? If yes: REGENERATE.
3. Is this a standalone 16:9 image, not a comparison grid or tutorial poster? If not: BLOCK.
4. Compared with other batch concepts, has this concept changed the action/story and the camera? If not: REGENERATE.
5. Is the corresponding video shot present or explicitly planned as a reconstruction? If not: SCENE_MATCH_PENDING (never PASS).


## SCALE MODIFIER (gắn thêm theo yêu cầu người dùng)

- **Level 1:** keep scale close to the reference, grounded and documentary.
- **Level 2:** make the log modestly larger in perceived scale while staying realistic.
- **Level 3:** make the log dramatically oversized for a strong thumbnail, still believable.
- **Level 4:** push to a colossal monumental scale using perspective, tiny workers, and a longer trailer.
- **Level 5:** maximum awe-inspiring scale exaggeration while preserving the exact identity of the reference log and a plausible transport setup.

Append one of these phrases near the beginning of each prompt when needed: “Use size amplification level X.”
